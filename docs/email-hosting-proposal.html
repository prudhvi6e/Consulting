2. Email: Open-source vs Zoho Mail vs Google Workspace

Three realistic ways to run @psrao.co.in mailboxes once the VPS is gone. Staff are comfortable with Gmail, so each option is also rated on "feels like Gmail".

A · Open-source

₹0 – 12,000/yr
₹0 routing · ₹8–12k self-hosted
  • A1 Cloudflare Email Routing + Gmail "Send mail as" — forward info@/career@ to existing Gmail accounts and reply from the @psrao.co.in address. ₹0.
  • A2 Self-hosted (Mailcow / Mail-in-a-Box / Zimbra) — your own mail server on a VPS. Server ₹8–12k/yr plus someone to maintain it.

B · Zoho Mail

₹0 – 4,500/yr
₹0 · ₹59/user/mo
  • Free: 5 users, 5 GB each, web + mobile app only (no IMAP/Outlook), one domain.
  • Mail Lite ₹59/user/mo (5 GB) or ₹75 (10 GB): IMAP, Outlook, multiple domains. 5 users = ₹3,540/yr.
  • Mail Premium ₹199/user/mo: 50 GB, archival, e-discovery.

C · Google Workspace

₹8,200/yr
₹137/user/mo
  • Business Starter (India, annual plan): 30 GB per user, Gmail, Meet (100), Drive, Docs/Sheets, shared calendars.
  • 5 users = ₹8,220/yr + GST (≈₹9,700). Monthly-flexible plan costs more; promos of ~₹350/mo often apply to the flexible plan.
  • Starter+ / Standard (2 TB, recording) ₹736/user/mo.

Recommendation. If ₹8–10k/year is acceptable, Google Workspace Business Starter — the team already works in Gmail and the firm owns the accounts. If the budget must stay minimal, Zoho Mail Lite (₹3,540/yr) gives real company mailboxes with Outlook/phone support. Zoho Free (₹0) works as a bridge for the 5 mailboxes while deciding. Self-hosting is the only option we advise against: the server costs as much as Zoho Lite before counting maintenance, and spam-filter reputation is a constant battle.
